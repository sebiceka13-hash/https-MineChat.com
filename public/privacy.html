Mine Chat — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

Mine Chat ("the app") is a multilingual AI chat assistant. This policy explains what data we collect, why, and the choices you have.

1. Data we collect

  • Account — when you sign in with Google: your name and email address.
  • Conversations — messages you send, together with the AI responses, so you can return to your chat history from any device. If you delete a conversation, we delete it.
  • Uploaded content — photos or documents you attach are sent to the AI to answer your question and are not kept afterwards.
  • Generated images — image prompts may be sent to third-party image services; images are sent back to you and are not stored by us.
  • Technical data — IP address and browser type, only for rate limiting and security, kept for a short time.

2. How we use it

  • To provide the chat, image generation and document analysis you ask for.
  • To keep you signed in on your devices.
  • To protect the service against abuse (rate limits, fraud prevention).

3. Cookies and similar storage

  • Cookies: session cookies keep you signed in and secure.
  • Local storage in your browser: your theme, language and logo preferences.

4. Third-party services

  • Google: sign in (OAuth 2.0) and the Gemini AI model that powers answers.
  • Image providers (e.g. Puter.js, Pollinations): generate images on request.
  • Hosting: your data is stored on our own server and in the services above.

We do not sell your personal data and do not show ads.

5. Data security

All traffic is encrypted (HTTPS). Access to your account requires your Google sign-in. We also apply rate limiting and security headers on the server.

6. Retention and deletion

Data is kept while your account is active. To delete your account or conversation history, contact us at the email below and we will remove it within a reasonable time.

7. Children

Mine Chat is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this page; the date above shows when it was last changed.
